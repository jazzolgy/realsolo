# Parker → Piano Solo Transfer Principles v0.1

## Purpose

Use Charlie Parker as the first high-resolution bebop melodic prior for Piano Player
without turning the piano into an alto-saxophone imitation system.

The transfer target is **decision grammar**, not literal phrases.

## Evidence layers

1. Existing Parker online profile
   - anticipation
   - passing / neighbor / close approach
   - directed altered color
   - resolution path
   - contextual triplet
   - syncopated long tone
   - ensemble space
   - wide-leap restraint

2. Uploaded Parker compilation audio study
   - strong local density variation
   - pulse speed != event density
   - many short phrase-space / handoff candidates
   - phrase space can preserve percussive activity while harmonic/melodic energy thins
   - deep-release and quiet-active space are measurably different

3. Piano-local realization
   - register
   - touch
   - right-hand feasibility
   - left-hand comping activity
   - pedal / articulation
   - interaction with accompaniment coverage

## Transfer rule

Do transfer:

- target-oriented chromatic motion
- phrase-entry timing tendencies
- anticipation
- connector logic
- directed tension/release
- contour discipline
- variable phrase density
- use of rests / space
- recovery after wide motion
- rhythmic re-entry over continuing pulse

Do not transfer literally:

- alto-saxophone register
- breath mechanics as if they were piano mechanics
- fingering/articulation constraints specific to saxophone
- exact recorded licks
- exact future note sequences
- fixed phrase length copied from a recording

## Bebop Core vs Parker Profile

### Shared Bebop Core candidate

Likely portable across later bebop masters:

- harmonic target awareness
- guide-tone arrival
- chromatic approach/enclosure behavior
- passing/neighbor connectivity
- anticipation across harmonic boundaries
- offbeat phrase entry
- phrase-density variation
- directed altered tension
- contour recovery
- silence as phrase structure

### Parker Profile

Keep Parker-specific only when evidence indicates a preference strength or conditional
frequency beyond generic bebop grammar.

Examples currently treated as profile tendencies:

- stronger preference for meaningful anticipation
- close chromatic connectors
- directed altered continuation
- syncopated long-tone life
- restraint against routine beat-1 long-tone repetition
- restraint against repeated wide leaps

These are biases, not mandatory rules.

## Piano realization

A piano solo candidate must pass two independent questions:

1. Is this a musically plausible bebop melodic decision?
2. Is this a convincing piano realization in the current ensemble texture?

A candidate can pass the Parker/bebop grammar and still be rejected locally because:

- register is poor for piano;
- left-hand comping is already dense;
- another instrument occupies the same register;
- ensemble density makes the line intrusive;
- touch/dynamic choice is wrong for the space;
- the phrase should remain open.

## Phrase-space behavior

Audio study now distinguishes:

### QUIET_ACTIVE

- energy falls;
- internal attack/pulse may remain;
- do not automatically fill;
- if re-entering over strong percussive support, pickup/anticipation may be favored.

### DEEP_RELEASE

- energy falls strongly;
- attack activity also falls;
- clearer phrase reset / re-entry opportunity;
- still no obligation to play immediately.

Actor attribution remains separate from the raw observation.

## Left-hand relationship

Piano solo and piano comping are not independent agents.

The right hand should know at least:

- current left-hand comping activity;
- harmonic coverage already supplied by left hand;
- rhythmic coverage already supplied by left hand;
- recent left-hand attack timing;
- current register overlap.

High left-hand activity should redirect right-hand creativity toward:

- clearer melodic shape;
- space;
- lighter density;
- register separation;
- selective chromatic targeting.

Low left-hand activity can permit:

- longer bebop lines;
- more active connector chains;
- more explicit harmonic outlining.

This is a coordination rule, not a fixed density map.

## Ensemble relationship

Parker-informed piano soloing must not become continuous foreground dominance.

The solo policy should remain sensitive to:

- drummer/percussive support;
- bass continuity;
- accompaniment role occupancy;
- phrase-space windows;
- ensemble response episodes;
- form/harmony transitions.

The goal is:

```
bebop melodic intelligence
+ piano-specific realization
+ ensemble listening
+ immediate creative response
```

## Runtime invariant

Even when a phrase intention spans several beats:

```
Plan intention, not notes.
```

Allowed:
- target function
- contour intention
- density direction
- register direction
- candidate connector family
- tension trajectory

Forbidden:
- freezing and replaying an exact future Parker-derived phrase
- literal recorded lick scheduling
- multi-beat exact-note scripts presented as improvisation
