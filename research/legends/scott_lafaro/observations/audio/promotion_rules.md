# Scott LaFaro phrase promotion rules

## Observation -> vocabulary candidate

Allowed when one phrase window has:
- verified source/personnel;
- phrase/form context;
- confidence >= 0.80 for the dimensions being stored;
- provenance;
- no guessed exact notes.

A one-off phrase may become vocabulary evidence. It does **not** become a
general LaFaro tendency merely because it is memorable.

## Observation -> tendency candidate

Require either:
- recurrence in at least two independent windows, or
- one repeated behavior across two verified recordings/tunes.

The recurring dimensions must be explicit, for example:
- register-trajectory behavior;
- rhythmic displacement;
- motif variation operation;
- delayed harmonic resolution;
- space after a phrase;
- interaction response.

## Tendency -> runtime profile

Require:
- context tags;
- bounded weight;
- confidence;
- provenance to the supporting observations;
- no exact future phrase.

## Copyright/similarity

Literal/transposed vocabulary is musically legitimate but is separately
controlled by Shared Vocabulary usage/similarity policy.

Prefer ABSTRACTED_PATTERN, FRAGMENT_RECALL, or HYBRID_COMPOSITION for public
runtime defaults until source rights/similarity policy says otherwise.
