# Sax application

Sax does not own Charlie Parker data.

The intended flow is:

Parker Research -> LegendProfileView + VocabularyQuery -> Shared Bebop/Harmony/Form/Reasoning -> Sax Policy -> Sax physical/articulation realization

Parker-specific register, breath-placement, leap-frequency, or
articulation/register tendencies belong to Parker Intelligence when they are
observed from Parker evidence. Generic fingering, breath capacity, altissimo
feasibility, articulation speed limits, and register-transition cost belong to
players/sax/.
