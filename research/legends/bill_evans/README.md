# Bill Evans Intelligence

Canonical research home for Bill Evans in RealSolo.

## Status

Bill Evans is now a first-class Legend Intelligence target.

This package starts **evidence-gated**. No musical tendency is promoted merely
from reputation, conventional jazz knowledge, or model memory. All runtime
priors must be traceable to the supplied Bill Evans recordings and score/form
comparison work.

## Research pipeline

SOURCE
-> OBSERVATION
-> POSITION-ALIGNED SCORE COMPARISON
-> VOCABULARY / ABSTRACTION
-> RUNTIME PRIOR

The immediate research emphasis is not only "Bill Evans piano style". It is the
role behavior of the whole trio at specific musical positions.

For every identified tune, compare the recording against the indexed score and
track role changes by form position:

- head / melody presentation
- repeated A/B sections
- solo chorus
- bass foreground
- piano foreground
- transitions
- out-head / ending

## Primary questions

### Piano
- Right-hand role: melody, variation, solo foreground, response, counterline, punctuation, space
- Left-hand role: voicing, pulse support, punctuation, bass complement, counter-motion, space
- Role change by form position
- register trajectory
- density and silence
- voicing/harmonic function
- motif development
- interaction with bass and drums

### Bass
- time carrier
- harmonic clarifier
- counter-melody
- direction setter
- response to piano
- solo foreground
- space / density adjustment

### Drums
- pulse maintenance
- setup / form marking
- phrase response
- density support or reduction
- soloist lift
- transition cue
- collective release

## Separation rule

Bill Evans-specific observations stay under Legend Intelligence.

Only behavior supported as more general trio grammar across multiple artists
may later move into Shared Jazz / Trio Grammar.

Generic piano, bass, and drum feasibility remains player-owned.

## Runtime ownership

Bill Evans data is consumed through shared LegendProfileView and VocabularyQuery.
No Player package should own Bill Evans-specific data.

## Source handling

Raw copyrighted/private recordings and scorebooks are not committed to the
public repository. Store manifests, provenance, observations, aggregate results,
and public-safe derived data only.


## Workstream ownership

To avoid duplicated Bill Evans work, use this split:

### Legend / shared research owns
- repeated listening across the full source set
- score-position alignment
- trio foreground/background role analysis
- Piano RH/LH role labels at the musical-intention level
- Bass and Drums role interaction
- Bill Evans-specific tendencies and vocabulary provenance
- deciding whether a recurring behavior stays Legend-specific or may be promoted to Shared Trio Grammar

### Piano Player owns
- piano-specific realization of shared/legend intentions
- hand distribution and playable RH/LH execution
- voicing realization details
- register realization inside piano constraints
- pedal, touch, articulation, and physical feasibility
- piano candidate generation/ranking and immediate commit behavior

### Handoff rule

Legend research may say **what role/intention Bill Evans evidence supports in this
context**. Piano decides **how a piano can realize that intention now**.

Do not duplicate Bill Evans source observations inside players/piano. If Piano
research discovers a potentially Bill Evans-specific musical behavior, send it
back to the Legend research layer for evidence/provenance validation before
promoting it as a legend prior.


## Boundary with Shared Solo Grammar

Bill Evans does not own general solo methodology.

Motif development, repetition/variation, fragmentation, rhythmic displacement,
space, register/density trajectory, future-harmony targeting, call/response,
and hybrid memory use belong to Shared Solo Grammar.

Bill Evans Intelligence stores evidence for **how Bill Evans uses those shared
operations**, including his conditional harmonic, rhythmic, melodic, and trio
interaction tendencies.
