# Trio Role Analysis

Primary research axis: **role by musical position**.

For each verified tune and form position, record:
- foreground / background ownership
- Piano RH role
- Piano LH role
- Bass role
- Drums role
- role handoff
- density change
- response delay
- form marker
- score deviation / interpretation
- confidence

Do not promote a Bill Evans-specific choice into Shared Trio Grammar until
cross-legend evidence supports the generalization.
