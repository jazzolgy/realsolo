# Piano application

Bill Evans-specific evidence belongs to Legend Intelligence, not players/piano.

Piano should consume Bill Evans through LegendProfileView and VocabularyQuery.
Generic piano feasibility and instrument policy remain player-owned.

A crucial analysis target is independent RH/LH role assignment by form position.


## Ownership handoff

Legend research provides:
- context/section role
- evidence-backed RH/LH intention labels
- Bill Evans-specific vocabulary/tendency references
- interaction constraints from Bass/Drums/ensemble context

Piano Player provides:
- actual voicing realization
- hand allocation
- physical/playability filtering
- register/pedal/touch/articulation realization
- immediate candidate selection and commit

Legend asks **what musical role should be expressed here?**
Piano answers **how should the piano physically and idiomatically realize it now?**
